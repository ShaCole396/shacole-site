Setting up ShaCole® App

A one-time setup for authorized ShaCole Group staff.

1. Add ShaCole® App in Google Chat

  1. Open Google Chat with your ShaCole Group account.
  2. Start a new direct message and search for ShaCole® App.
  3. Select the app and confirm Add / Install.

2. Authorize the app

The first time you message ShaCole® App, Google will ask you to authorize it for your account. Review the requested access and approve it. This is a one-time step per user. See our Privacy Policy for what the app accesses and why.

3. Run your first command

  1. Type /lookup <client name or Deal ID> and send.
  2. ShaCole® App returns a client card with status and quick actions.
  3. Type /help any time to see all commands.

Need access?

ShaCole® App is a private application for ShaCole® Group Inc. staff. If you can’t find it or need to be granted access, contact your administrator at support@shacole.com.
