Help & Getting Started

Using the ShaCole® App inside Google Chat.

Start a chat with ShaCole® App

  1. Open Google Chat and start a new direct message.
  2. Search for ShaCole® App and select the app.
  3. The first time, you’ll be asked to authorize the app for your account — approve it to continue.

Everyday commands

  1. /lookup <name or ID> — returns a client card with status, contact details, and quick actions.
  2. /report — a business overview and referral snapshot.
  3. /help — shows the current list of commands in Chat.

Tips

  • You can look up by a client’s name or by their Deal ID.
  • Card buttons run as you; some actions may be routed for review depending on your role.
