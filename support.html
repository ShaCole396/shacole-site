What the App does

ShaCole® App is an internal Google Chat app that lets authorized staff look up clients, view their status, and run quick reports over the company’s records — without needing direct access to the underlying spreadsheet.

Common commands

  • /lookup <name or ID> — pull up a client card.
  • /report — an at-a-glance business overview.
  • /help — list the available commands inside Chat.

Getting the App

ShaCole® App is a private application available only to ShaCole® Group Inc. staff. If you need access, contact your administrator at support@shacole.com.
