What to include

To help us resolve it quickly, please include:

  • What you were trying to do (e.g. the command you ran, such as /lookup).
  • What happened instead, and the exact wording of any error.
  • Roughly when it happened.
  • A screenshot, if you have one.

Please don’t include passwords or sensitive personal information in your report.
