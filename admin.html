Managing ShaCole® for ShaCole Group Inc.

ShaCole® is a private Google Workspace application, available only within ShaCole Group Inc. It is not offered to the public and cannot be installed by users outside the organization.

Installing the app for your team

A ShaCole Group Workspace administrator manages availability from the Google Admin console:

  1. Go to Apps → Google Workspace Marketplace apps.
  2. Locate ShaCole® and choose who can install it — the whole organization, or specific organizational units or groups.
  3. Team members can then add ShaCole® in Google Chat and authorize it for their own account.

Access & scopes

The app requests only the Google Workspace scopes it needs to function (Sheets, Gmail, Calendar, Contacts, Drive, Tasks, and Chat). See the Privacy Policy for details. Each user authorizes the app once for their own account.

Revoking access

Administrators can remove or restrict the app at any time from the Admin console, and individual users can be removed from the allowed audience. Removing the app immediately ends its access to Workspace data.
